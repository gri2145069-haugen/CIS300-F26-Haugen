Welcome to my site!

This is a detailed overview of a portion of my Transformers collection. The series I will be covering is Transformers Cybertron from the year 2005.

I have been collecting Transformers on and off for over 21 years and currently own more than 400 figures. I wanted to share some of my collection because one particular series played a significant role in my childhood.

The Background tab tells my collecting story while mentioning the significant moments in my collection journey. In the Collection tab, you will find information about the figures in my collection. The Market tab provides current market values and pricing history for these figures. If you have any questions about the figures or Transformers in general, feel free to visit the Questions tab and ask away. I will do my best to answer them.
