This page provides information on the current market value ranges for the Transformers I own from the Cybertron toyline. The prices are based on recent sales of figures in similar condition and completion on Ebay. I use Ebay because it is the primary site used for sales of older figures.

There is also information on the figures I own from the Transformers Cybertron toyline. These tables are divided into both factions of either Autobots or Decepticons. They then procede to show the figures name, price point, the completition percentage, and year produced. The price point is around what you could sell the figure for currently in the market and is based off of recent Ebay sales of similar condition figures and same completion. The completion percentage is found by assessing how many seperate pieces a figure can split into and how many external accessoires they have. This total sum of pieces are then divided by 100 to arrive at a percentage of what each piece is worth. You then take into consideration how many pieces are missing and subtract that from the total to arrive at the completition percentage.
