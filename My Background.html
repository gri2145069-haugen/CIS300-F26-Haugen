In 2005 I was 4 years old and my uncle wanting to show me one of his passions, introduced me to the world of The Transformers by buying me two figures (Bumblebee & Starscream) from the Robots in Disguise Classics line that was in stores at the time. From there I saw commercials on Cartoon Network in 2006 for the new show Transformers Cybertron and got very excited to watch it due to its up-beat electronic soundtrack and cool character desings. I then started seeing those toys at stores and bought a couple and overtime I got a few more from family members for birthday’s and holidays. Even at school me and my friends would bring our Transformers so at recess we could play with each others different figures. There were even times where on Fridays we would trade toys for the weekend and bring them back on Monday so we could enjoy them for that period of time. I can say I grew up on this show and once I got hooked on this series I was telling my uncle about it and he then asked “Do you want to see the original series from 1984 when I was a kid?” I told him yes and he then started me on the first episode of the Transformers. The series was a cartoon which followed the Heroic Autobots leaving their war tarnished home planet of Cybertron to search the universe to find resources to convert into their planets energy source known as “Energon”. Unfortunately, the Autobots were followed upon leaving Cybertron by the Evil Decepticons who wanted to destroy the Autobots and anyone who stood in their way of controlling the universe for ultimate power. I thought the cartoon was awesome and in my excitement my uncle showed me a few of his figures from the 80’s and showed me how to Transform them and from that defining point I was hooked.

Just two years after my introduction to Transformers the first live-action movie came out in theatres. It was different from the other incarnations of Transformers I already knew but, I still found this new style of the characters, different story and all the explosions to be sweet! The additions of new characters and redesigns of ones I knew had me begging my parents for some of the figures. One of the first scenes in the movie is where the Decepticon Blackout Transforms and attacks the Military Operations Base set in Qatar and it gave me chills and was probably the coolest scene in all of Transformers live-action just because of how intense it was as an introduction to the type of violence the Decepticons could ensue on the Earth. I also got numerous figures from this movie and the following movies after yes, including the voyager class Blackout. The second and third live- action movies were good and during that time period there were many very good toys released in correlation with those movies. Then I took a few year hiatus from 2014-2017 where I wasn’t following Transformers or really collecting. To my knowledge this time period was sort of a dead period where not much was going on with the franchise from a toy standpoint. In June of 2017 I went to see the fourth Transformers movie and that got me back into Transformers but, upon seeing the lack of effort and quality of the toys I decided to opt. out of collecting those toys. From here I only looked at figures online and got a few for holidays.

The big shift in my collecting journey happened in September of 2019. I had just gotten my first job at a sandwich shop a few months earlier and I was looking on Craigslist of all places and found a Generation One Combiner called Predaking for a really good deal. Long story short I messaged the owner and we met at a Target parking lot. I was $180 out but, I was extremely happy to not only find but own a piece of my childhood. This then started a spark (Pun intended) in me to collect not only generation one figures but go and collect as many of the Transformers from the Cybertron toyline I grew up with as well. To add to all that, in 2018 a line came out called Studio Series. This line was based around the live-action franchise and the idea was for these figures to be as screen accurate as possible, despite their complex designs. So I went ahead and started collecting these as well. There were other releases with unique or never done before odds and ends figures that I have picked up as well because of their significance or screen accuracy. It is now October of 2026 and I am still collecting at a steady pace. As of right now I have collected 400+ figures and don’t plan on stopping anytime soon.
